Teacher guide

  • Simple uses fewer choices and extra cues. Medium adds choices and tiles. Difficult uses closer choices and the full alphabet in Spelling Forge.
  • Echo Tower is for hearing a target sound. Blend Bridge asks students to pull the lever, say the sounds, and read the whole word. The lever starts the mic after browser permission. The meter measures volume. Keep the sound at or above the star continuously for 1.5 seconds to pass. The teacher checks pronunciation before moving on. No speech-recognition service is required. Use No microphone — teacher check when needed.
  • Sound Potions asks students to listen, say each sound aloud, and tap the matching tiles in order. Later words add extra tiles, with more distractors on Difficult. A tile can have more than one letter, such as sh. Pouring checks the order.
  • Spelling Forge starts with two words to finish, then asks children to spell whole words with more letters to choose from. Finish a set and strike the anvil to forge part of the magic sword. The Sorting Hall has students hear a word, choose its sound group, then read the revealed words at the end. One timer covers the whole round: 18 seconds on Simple, 20 on Medium, and 20 on Difficult. It pauses during audio and can be switched off. Sentence Quest asks students to find words that share a sound or spelling, then read the restored story.
  • Whack-a-Mole compares target sounds or asks students to catch a deliberate spelling mistake. Spelling mode explains the correction, including ch/tch, ck, dge and FLOSS rules. Flashlight Library has children reveal words only under the beam and collect a target sound, or a target sound with a named spelling. Successful rounds advance automatically; Pause gives time for teacher discussion. In the final reading review, the microphone measures volume; after the sound stays at or above the star for 1.5 seconds, the highlighted book flies to its shelf. Background music is off during reading. Teacher check is available without a microphone. The teacher checks reading accuracy. Open these games below the academy map. Each has its own focus menu. Medium and Difficult draw from broader word banks. Use Help me compare to pause. Saved child recordings are available for the original words; teachers can read the extra words aloud.
  • For projection, have students say the word or explain the rule before selecting. Use Teacher word if you prefer to read an audio prompt yourself.

Collect a room rune by getting at least two-thirds of a full round correct. You can play every room at any time.
