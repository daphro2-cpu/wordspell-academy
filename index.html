Teacher guide

  • Simple uses fewer choices and extra cues. Medium adds choices and tiles. Difficult uses closer choices and the full alphabet in Spelling Forge.
  • Echo Tower is for hearing a target sound. Blend Bridge asks students to pull the lever, say the sounds, and read the whole word. The mic checks which word the browser heard. A teacher can check each sound and approve the word. Some browsers send audio to their speech service.
  • Sound Potions asks students to say each sound aloud and add one drop per sound. Check that the child says the sounds in order.
  • Spelling Forge gives children several words to repair with a letter bank. Finish a set and strike the anvil to forge part of the magic sword. The Sorting Hall has students clear clouds and sort several words by sound or spelling. Sentence Quest asks students to find words that share a sound or spelling, then read the restored story.
  • For projection, have students say the word or explain the rule before selecting. Use Teacher word if you prefer to read an audio prompt yourself.
